Accessibility overlay scan results

This site lists public websites that were submitted for review and whether their HTML references a known accessibility overlay product. It records the dates those findings change and the overlay asset versions it can see.

A finding is a signal, not a verdict. An overlay reference does not prove a site is inaccessible, and a missing reference does not prove it is accessible. This project does not test WCAG conformance.

Known concerns with overlays

These references explain why overlay products are controversial. They are third-party sources, not findings by this project, and they are not legal advice.

General context

  • Overlay Fact Sheet

    Community statement signed by accessibility practitioners explaining why overlay products do not make a site accessible.

  • Illinois DOIT: Accessibility overlays

    Government guidance on the limits of overlay products and the need for structural remediation.

  • FTC: accessiBe final order (provider example)

    In 2025 the FTC approved a final order requiring accessiBe to pay $1 million over deceptive claims that its AI product could make websites compliant with accessibility guidelines. This is one example of a provider-specific action, not a finding about any site listed here.

Request a review

Submit a site through the scan request form. Requests require confirmation that you are authorised to ask for the scan and that you understand the results will be public. A maintainer reviews requests before they enter the scan list.

To correct a finding, open a regular issue with the site URL and the evidence you want reviewed.

Method and limitations

  • The scanner fetches the initial HTML only; it does not run JavaScript.
  • An overlay injected at runtime, or referenced only from another script, may be missed.
  • Version detection is best-effort and may be blank or wrong.
  • HTTP errors and timeouts are reported as statuses, not treated as proof of anything.
  • Only sites submitted through the issue form or curated by maintainers are scanned.
